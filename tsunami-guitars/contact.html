Before You Buy

Common Questions

How do you pack and ship a guitar?

+

Every guitar is packed properly — bubble wrap, styrofoam, paper, and cardboard in a proper guitar box. We've shipped dozens of instruments without a single damage claim. We use Canada Post, UPS, and FedEx depending on destination and size, and every shipment is fully insured. You can buy with confidence that it will arrive exactly as described.

Do you ship internationally?

+

Yes — we ship worldwide. We're based in Calgary, Alberta, and our buyers come from across Canada, the United States, Europe, and beyond. If you're outside Canada, just reach out before purchasing and we'll confirm shipping costs and timeline for your location.

How long does shipping take?

+

Most guitars arrive within 1–2 weeks depending on your location. Canadian and US buyers are typically on the faster end of that range. International shipments can take a little longer. We'll keep you updated with tracking from the moment it leaves our hands.

Do you offer payment plans?

+

Yes — payment plans are available through Reverb. If you're buying direct and want to discuss options, just reach out. We'd rather work something out than see a great guitar go to the wrong home.

How do you verify authenticity?

+

Years of collecting, studying, and handling these instruments. We know the headstocks, the serial number formats, the hardware, the wood grain, the weight — all of it. Every guitar we sell has been personally inspected and verified. If something doesn't add up, we don't list it. It's that simple.

What do your condition grades mean?

+

Player's Grade — this guitar has been played and it shows. Dings, dents, and marks that tell the story of a life well-lived. Nothing structural, nothing that affects playability — just honest wear from years of use. These are often the best-sounding guitars we carry.

Excellent — very little play wear. May have the odd small mark under close inspection but nothing that stands out. These guitars look and play like instruments that were treated with care.

Near Mint / Mint — essentially unplayed. Exactly as it left the factory, often with original plastics still intact.

What if the guitar isn't as described?

+

Absolutely — if a guitar arrives not as described, we'll make it right. Full stop. Our Reverb 5-star record exists because we describe everything accurately and stand behind every sale. We're not a faceless marketplace — you're buying directly from Derek, and that means something.

Can you find a specific guitar for me?

+

Of course. If you're hunting something specific — a particular Navigator, a certain Tokai model, a Crews Maniac Sound or anything else from Japan — reach out and tell us what you're after. We source specifically for collectors on our list when we can. The worst we can say is we haven't seen one lately.
