At 8.8 lbs it has the weight and density that serious players associate with sustain and authority. Comes in its genuine Navigator hard case. These were always rare. Now they're rarer.

Heritage Wood · Ancient Tonewood

2011 · Made in Japan · FujiGen · History Guitars · Heritage Wood

History Les Paul Heritage Wood

Sunburst · Flame Maple Top · Ancient Great Lakes Tonewood

The Heritage Wood stamp on the back of this headstock is a designation most players will walk right past — and serious collectors will stop cold for. The tonewoods in this guitar were sourced from ancient logs recovered from the Great Lakes, some of them hundreds of years old, already dried and aged beyond what any commercial supplier could dream of offering. Wood that old resonates differently. It sings at frequencies freshly milled timber simply cannot access. FujiGen built this 2011 Les Paul exclusively for a single Japanese retailer — never exported, never mass-marketed — and finished it in a sunburst over a stunning flame maple top that catches light like amber. At 7.6 lbs it sits in the sweetest weight range for a Les Paul: substantial enough for sustain, light enough to play all night without thinking about it. This is what it feels like when the wood, the builder, and the spec all arrive at the same place at the same time.
